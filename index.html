About EveryLock

EveryLock is an Android privacy application designed to protect personal photos, videos, and selected apps.

Vault media is stored in encrypted form on the user's device. EveryLock also supports encrypted recovery backups using the user's own Google Drive account.

Key Features

🔐 PIN and biometric protection

🖼️ Encrypted photo vault

🎬 Encrypted video vault

📱 Optional app locking

☁️ Encrypted Google Drive recovery backup

🔒 AES-256 authenticated encryption

Google Drive Backup

EveryLock uses Google Drive only when the user chooses to connect their Google account for encrypted backup and recovery.

The app requests access only to its private Google Drive appDataFolder.

EveryLock does not request access to browse, read, or modify the user's normal Google Drive files.

Backup media is encrypted before being stored in Google Drive.
