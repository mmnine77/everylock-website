EveryLock Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 17, 2026

EveryLock is designed to help users protect private photos, videos, and selected applications on Android devices.

This Privacy Policy explains how EveryLock handles information and how Google Drive access is used.

1. Local Vault Data

Photos and videos added to the EveryLock Vault are stored in encrypted form within the application's private storage on the user's device.

Vault content is protected using authenticated encryption and is not intentionally uploaded to an external service unless the user enables encrypted recovery backup.

2. Google Account and Google Drive

EveryLock allows users to optionally connect a Google account for encrypted recovery backup.

EveryLock requests the following Google Drive OAuth scope:

https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata

This permission allows EveryLock to access only its private appDataFolder in the user's Google Drive.

EveryLock does not request permission to browse or access the user's normal Google Drive documents, photos, folders, or other personal files.

3. Encrypted Recovery Backups

If the user enables Google Drive recovery backup, EveryLock uploads encrypted backup data to the application's private Google Drive appDataFolder.

Backup media remains encrypted. Recovery requires the credentials and recovery information configured by the user.

EveryLock is designed so that possession of the Google account alone is not sufficient to decrypt protected Vault content.

4. Recovery Password

The Recovery Password is used to protect recovery key material used for encrypted backup restoration.

Users are responsible for remembering their Recovery Password.

5. App Lock Accessibility Service

EveryLock may optionally use Android Accessibility Service functionality to detect when a protected application is opened and display the EveryLock lock interface.

The service is designed for package-level app locking and is not intended to read messages, passwords, text fields, or screen contents.

Accessibility-based App Lock is optional and can be disabled by the user through Android settings.

6. Biometric Authentication

EveryLock may use Android's biometric authentication system to allow supported users to unlock the Vault.

Biometric authentication is handled by Android. EveryLock does not receive or store fingerprint images or raw biometric data.

7. Information Sharing

EveryLock does not sell users' Vault photos or videos.

Encrypted Vault backup information is transferred to the Google Drive account chosen by the user only when the backup feature is used.

8. Data Deletion

Users can remove local Vault items from within the application.

Uninstalling EveryLock or clearing its application data may permanently remove locally stored Vault data that has not been backed up or exported.

Google Drive recovery data remains associated with the user's Google account until removed through supported backup-management functionality or applicable Google account controls.

9. Security

EveryLock uses encryption and Android security mechanisms to protect Vault information. However, no software or storage system can guarantee absolute security.

10. Children's Privacy

EveryLock is not specifically designed to knowingly collect personal information from children.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when EveryLock features or privacy practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.
